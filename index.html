02Why I design

Good design isn't about adding more. It's about understanding what deserves to stay.

Every project starts with curiosity.

I collect ideas, business goals, technical constraints, edge cases, user frustrations and assumptions before I ever think about layouts.

Only after everything is understood do I begin removing.

Because clarity isn't designed.It's uncovered.

03Selected work

Two problems that changed how I work.

Featured case study

Mojaz Report Landing Page

GoGoMotor · 2026

The problem

A detail page can only verify a car already in inventory. A VIN that matches nothing — a private seller, a friend's car, another site entirely — has nowhere to go, so that visitor leaves.

The observation

The VIN field already accepted any car. The retention behaviour existed — the page just never said so.

The solution

One standalone page doing two jobs: catching a lookup that would have left, and capturing search demand a detail page can never reach.

38.4k monthly organic sessions·18% customer conversion
Read the case study ↗

Bay Management

Rampwin — for Petromin · 2025

The problem

A service floor running on a whiteboard and three WhatsApp groups. Nobody knew which bay was free without walking over to look.

The observation

Two days on the floor and nobody once asked me where a part was. They asked whether they could promise the car by five.

The solution

Every bay a card, every card a live state. And no filters — because if you need to filter a floor you can see, the layout is wrong.

31% faster turnaround·Onboarding 2 days → 20 min·projected

07About me

I didn't become a designer through design school. I became one through curiosity.

I'm self-taught.

That meant learning by building, breaking things, asking questions, making mistakes, and doing it all again.

Every project became a classroom. Every mistake became part of the next solution.

That mindset still shapes how I approach design today — I never learned to stop checking whether I was wrong.
