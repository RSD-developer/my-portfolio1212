Mojaz Report Landing Page

The context

The detail page already solves half the problem

On every used-car detail page, trust is already handled: a free Mojaz report — with an option to buy the full version — lets a buyer verify that specific car's history without ever leaving the page.

01

Trust, handled

A free preview alongside a paid, complete report gives a buyer everything needed to verify one car, right where they're already looking.

02

Identification, handled

The VIN is already attached to the detail page the buyer is viewing — the report just needs to be surfaced, not looked up.

The gap

Not every VIN belongs to a car in inventory

A private seller's car, a dealership not on the platform, a friend's car, a car listed on another site entirely — the detail-page flow has no way to serve any of those requests.

The break

Without a place to check an unlisted VIN, that visitor leaves the site — for Mojaz's own site, or a competitor. The experience breaks at the exact moment GoGoMotor could have kept them, with no guarantee they come back.

The opportunity

One page, two jobs

Retention

Gives GoGoMotor somewhere to send — or catch — a visitor whose VIN doesn't match any car in inventory, instead of losing that visit to a competitor or to Mojaz's own site.

Traffic

Mojaz is an established, trusted brand in Saudi Arabia. A page built around it can also capture visitors who start from search, not from browsing GoGoMotor — demand a detail page can never reach.

The design

From brief to shipped screen

Three moments carry the whole flow: capture the VIN, show what the report contains, then turn either kind of visitor into a GoGoMotor session.

Works for any VIN — but doesn't say so

The field accepts any VIN — exactly the retention behavior needed — but nothing tells a visitor this works even for a car GoGoMotor doesn't have listed. "Powered by Mojaz" borrows a trusted, recognized brand — useful for a retained visitor and a newly acquired one alike.

Engagement for both audiences

A free option matters more here than on a detail page — a visitor who almost left needs a low-friction reason to stay, not an immediate paywall. The sticky bar repeats both CTAs on scroll — reused componentry, not a bespoke build.

Where retention becomes a session

Coverage and confidence numbers reassure; the News and Used Cars links are where either kind of visitor actually becomes a GoGoMotor session — a visitor who almost left ends up browsing inventory, and a brand-new visitor gets a first look at everything else GoGoMotor offers.

Landing page & offer decisions

Four calls worth naming

01

Reuse over invention

Pill buttons, lavender cards, the Outfit/purple system — all pre-existing. Effort belongs in the offer and messaging, not new UI.

02

Freemium as the stay-on-site wedge

For a visitor who almost bounced to a competitor, free is the lowest-friction reason to stay on GoGoMotor at all.

03

Borrowed proof, not built proof

Mojaz's own trust numbers work for an unfamiliar visitor and one who already trusts the brand but needs reassurance about an unlisted car.

04

An unscoped field, with no unscoped message

The VIN field already does the retention job — it accepts any car. What's missing is the page ever saying so.

Takeaways

One page, two jobs, one insight

01

A single page can retain a visit that would otherwise leave, and acquire one that never would have arrived — without needing two different builds.

02

The most valuable behavior a page has can go completely unstated. Accepting any VIN does the retention job either way — but saying so out loud would do it better.

03

The real point of leverage isn't the report — it's the moment right after it, where a visitor who could have left instead clicks into the rest of the site.
